# Music — credits

**Composed in code for Bizzing.** Every loop in Bizzing India is written in
[`engine.js`](engine.js) as a raga, a tala and a tune, and played live in the browser by
instruments built from Web Audio oscillators: a tanpura drone, a bansuri, a santoor, a
sarangi, a tabla, a dholak, a dhak and manjira. There are no recordings and no audio files,
so there is nothing licensed from anyone.

The ragas and talas are traditional and belong to everyone; what is borrowed is only the
scale of notes and the cycle of beats. No melody is taken from any composition — each tune
is generated from its theme's own seed, the same every time, and is not a performance of a
raga in the classical sense. Each loop is 60–90 seconds and seamless.

The narration is unchanged: the recorded voices described in `docs/` are the only recordings
in the app, and no new narration has been added.
